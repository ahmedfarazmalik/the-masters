How to Order

  1. Choose your food from the menu and tap Add to Cart.
  2. Open your cart and fill in your name, numbers, delivery area and address.
  3. Tap Place Order on WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens with your order already written.
  4. Press Send in WhatsApp. The restaurant will check your order and confirm it with you.

Your order is not placed until you send the WhatsApp message. Payment is Cash on Delivery.
